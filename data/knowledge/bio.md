# About Cory Carmen

Cory Carmen is a junior full-stack software developer based on Vancouver Island,
British Columbia, Canada, and originally from Powell River, BC. He focuses on
building practical, user-focused web and mobile applications with the MERN stack
(MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js).

Cory was born on June 27, 1971. He identifies as male and uses he/him pronouns.

## Career transition

Cory's path into software development is not a traditional one. Before entering
tech, he spent 25 years in the commercial diving and fishing industry, where
safety, precision, teamwork, and problem-solving were part of everyday life.
That work taught him to adapt quickly, stay calm under pressure, and keep
learning through hands-on experience.

As a commercial diver and fisherman, Cory harvested geoducks, both red and green
sea urchins, and sea cucumbers. He also carried out many different scientific
research dive surveys for Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO).

Cory's move into software came after an injury brought his diving career to an
end. He retrained through WorkSafeBC, choosing software development because it is
far less physically demanding and because computers are something he has always
genuinely enjoyed. What he loves most is creating and troubleshooting software —
building something and then working through the problems to make it run well.

He now brings the same discipline and persistence to debugging, building
projects, and learning new technologies that he brought to his previous career.

## Education

Cory graduated in 2026 from the Software Development program at UBC through
CircuitStream. He holds a Circuit Stream Certified Software Developer credential
(verifiable on Credly).

## What he is focused on

Cory is currently focused on full-stack developer roles and projects, and on
deepening his skills in React, Express.js, MongoDB, and authentication
workflows. He is available for junior developer, freelance, and internship
roles. His longer-term goal is to grow into a senior full-stack developer.

## Strengths

Adaptability, staying calm under pressure, persistence with debugging, teamwork,
and a hands-on, practical approach to learning — carried over from a long career
in demanding commercial marine environments.

## Work style

Cory approaches software much the way he approached commercial diving: calmly,
carefully, and with close attention to detail. He plans before diving in, stays
composed when things go wrong, breaks large problems into smaller manageable
steps, and keeps troubleshooting patiently until the job is done right.

## Why work with Cory

What makes Cory stand out is the combination of a fresh, up-to-date full-stack
skill set with 25 years of real-world discipline. He is used to high-stakes
environments where safety, precision, and problem-solving matter, and he brings
that same focus and reliability to writing and shipping code.

He does not just learn technologies in isolation — he builds and deploys complete,
working projects with them. His capstone, StockGrader, is a fully deployed
full-stack app with authentication, third-party data, and a full suite of unit
tests across the frontend and backend, which he is especially proud of. Cory also values testing and quality: he writes unit
tests with Vitest to make sure his code works as expected.

Cory is a motivated junior developer who is eager to keep growing, takes feedback
well, and is dependable, curious, and genuinely committed to a long-term career
in software development.

Outside of software development, Cory enjoys camping, fishing, hiking, scuba
